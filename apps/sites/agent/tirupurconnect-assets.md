# Tirupur Connect textile artwork

The active website uses original AI-created illustrative textile studies. These images do not depict listed businesses, actual Tirupur facilities, or verified products.

## Delivery files

- `textile-hero.webp`: portrait cotton knit and yarn still life, 1000 by 1500 pixels.
- `textile-collection.webp`: six equal square textile studies, arranged in a 1536 by 1024 pixel contact sheet.
- `industry-collection.webp`: six equal square industry studies, arranged in a 1536 by 1024 pixel contact sheet.

Category panels display individual contact-sheet tiles through CSS background positions. Every tile retains square proportions. The two collections cover all twelve discovery categories.

The built-in image generation tool produced the original images. WebP compression reduces delivery size without changing their composition. The three delivery files total approximately 906 KB.

## Prompt set

### Textile collection

Use case: product-mockup. Asset type: premium Tirupur Connect textile website category photography collection. Create one refined editorial contact sheet with exactly three columns and two rows. All six tiles have equal square proportions, no gutters, borders, text, letters, or logos. Each tile is a complete independent composition with a centered subject and breathing room. Use a warm ivory studio background, soft natural window light, forest green, cream, terracotta, and sage. Subjects, in reading order: unbleached cotton yarn cones with loose thread; folded forest-green jersey with visible knit detail; terracotta dyed cloth draped over a cream ceramic cylinder; cream and sage T-shirts on wooden hangers; ivory cloth with a forest-green embroidered curve and embroidery hoop; kraft garment packaging with cotton labels, a wooden button, and natural twine. Use tactile realistic material texture. Avoid people, facility claims, 3D graphics, and website UI.

### Hero

Use case: product-mockup. Asset type: portrait hero photograph for Tirupur Connect. Create a studio editorial still life of sculptural folded cotton knits in forest green, ivory, sage, and terracotta. Place two cotton yarn cones behind the stack and let jersey flow off an ivory limestone pedestal. Use a portrait 2:3 composition, the material stack in the lower two thirds, negative space at the upper right, and soft window light from the left. Show knit stitches and natural folds. Use a warm ivory background and a calm, controlled composition. Avoid people, buildings, real facility claims, text, logos, watermarks, UI, and 3D illustration.

### Industry collection

Use case: product-mockup. Asset type: coordinated image collection for a textile industry website. Create a contact sheet with exactly three columns and two rows of equal square tiles. Use six independent compositions in the same warm ivory studio, forest green and sage palette, natural cotton texture, and soft window light. Subjects, in reading order: a sewing-machine needle stitching ivory cotton; a magnifying inspection glass, metal fabric gauge, and knit swatches; a neutral and sage swatch fan beside a blank notebook; kraft shipping cartons with cotton straps; recycled cotton fibres, a sage yarn cone, and a small leaf; an unbranded laptop seen from behind beside blank paper and a cotton sample book. Give every subject breathing room. Avoid people, facility claims, text, logos, UI, and 3D illustration.
