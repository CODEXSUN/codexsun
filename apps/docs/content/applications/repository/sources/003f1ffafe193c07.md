# Marketing

## Status

Planning baseline: 2026-10-23. Phase 1 implementation is in progress locally.
The tenant foundation, capture intake, and CRM handoff now have API and web source.
Local checks and remaining work are recorded in [TASK.md](agent/TASK.md#local-implementation-and-verification-log).

## Purpose

Provide each client with the same Digital Marketing dashboard, backed by that client's own tenant database.
Clients manage their own brands, social accounts, campaigns, email campaigns, lead capture, and approved automation.
CRM manages sales qualification, enquiries, assignments, and sales follow-up through public integration contracts.

One shared application serves many tenants. Do not create a code fork or separate dashboard implementation for each client.
Keep business records, credentials, files, caches, exports, jobs, and connector activity isolated by tenant.
Tenant-specific brands, settings, channels, templates, and permissions customize the shared experience.

## Supported planning scope

- Facebook Pages, Meta Lead Ads, Instagram professional accounts, and WhatsApp Business.
- LinkedIn, Google Ads lead forms, Google Business Profile, YouTube, Threads, Pinterest, X, and Telegram.
- Email campaigns, audience segments, templates, approval, scheduling, unsubscribe, suppression, and delivery reporting.
- Campaign tracking, Sites landing-page forms, controlled imports, CRM handoff, and outcome attribution.
- A content calendar, media library, supported social inbox, manual publishing tasks, and integration health.
- Durable automation with consent checks, stop rules, human takeover, and audited execution.

TikTok is excluded. A separate future Super Admin app may manage multiple clients from one place.
The tenant Marketing app must not contain an all-client dashboard or cross-tenant reporting API.
Provider APIs expose different capabilities. A connected account does not imply publishing, messaging, lead retrieval, or analytics access.

## Promotional email eligibility

Marketing owns `marketing_consent_events`. Grants and withdrawals are immutable evidence entries, not editable subscriptions.
Each entry binds the current company, permitted brand, normalized recipient email, promotional purpose, notice version, source, evidence reference, and dates.
Identical request keys replay the same entry. Changed payloads conflict. Re-consent requires later explicit evidence.
Inactive brands block grants and eligibility. Authorized withdrawals remain possible for inactive brands.
Privacy consent and imports never create promotional grants automatically.

CRM owns `crm_contact_restrictions` and its append-only do-not-contact/release evidence.
Core supplies all matching email identity IDs without merging contacts.
CRM also checks existing Contact Preferences across every matching identity, including inactive records.
Any nonempty legacy permission other than the explicit value `allowed` requires review.
An operational `allowed` preference does not replace Marketing consent.
Mail owns append-only `mail_suppressions` for hard bounce, complaint, and delivery policy evidence.
Marketing grants cannot clear Mail blocks. Suppression release and authenticated provider feedback remain future work.

`POST /marketing/consents/eligibility` reads the current three authorities through server-composed contracts.
Unavailable, disabled, unauthorized, or unmigrated owners block eligibility. Consent changes during the check also block it.
Results describe the check time only. Future dispatch must repeat the checks before enqueue and execution.
The local CODEXSUN registry currently lacks a Mail entitlement. Its eligibility remains blocked until Mail access is explicitly configured.
This continuation preserves that existing activation state.

Apply the additive Marketing consent, CRM restriction, and Mail suppression migrations and permission seeds before tenant use.
Local CODEXSUN received these changes after verified SQL backups. No other tenant activation or customer sample data changed.
Consent read/create, CRM restriction check/manage, and Mail suppression check/record use separate permissions.
Only the tenant Admin receives these new grants by default.
The Consents page provides list, search-toolbar filters/columns, evidence recording, Show, activity, and a fresh eligibility check.
CRM restriction and Mail safety writes use their owner APIs. They do not send messages or call a provider.
