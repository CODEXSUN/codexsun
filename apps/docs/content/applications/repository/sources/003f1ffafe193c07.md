# Marketing

## Status

Planning baseline: 2026-10-23. This component contains documentation only.
No Marketing API, UI, migrations, connectors, permissions, workers, or deployment exists from this plan.
An unchecked task is planned work. Provider access and live behavior remain unverified.

## Purpose

Provide each client with the same Digital Marketing dashboard, backed by that client's own tenant database.
Clients manage their own brands, social accounts, campaigns, email campaigns, lead capture, and approved automation.
CRM manages sales qualification, enquiries, assignments, and sales follow-up through public integration contracts.

One shared application serves many tenants. Do not create a code fork or separate dashboard implementation for each client.
Keep business records, credentials, files, caches, exports, jobs, and connector activity isolated by tenant.
Tenant-specific brands, settings, channels, templates, and permissions customize the shared experience.

## Supported planning scope

- Facebook Pages, Meta Lead Ads, Instagram professional accounts, and WhatsApp Business.
- LinkedIn, Google Ads lead forms, Google Business Profile, YouTube, Threads, Pinterest, X, and Telegram.
- Email campaigns, audience segments, templates, approval, scheduling, unsubscribe, suppression, and delivery reporting.
- Campaign tracking, Sites landing-page forms, controlled imports, CRM handoff, and outcome attribution.
- A content calendar, media library, supported social inbox, manual publishing tasks, and integration health.
- Durable automation with consent checks, stop rules, human takeover, and audited execution.

TikTok is excluded. A separate future Super Admin app may manage multiple clients from one place.
The tenant Marketing app must not contain an all-client dashboard or cross-tenant reporting API.
Provider APIs expose different capabilities. A connected account does not imply publishing, messaging, lead retrieval, or analytics access.

## Ownership and dependencies

| Owner     | Contract with Marketing                                                                                                                |
| --------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Marketing | Own campaigns, brands, social connections, content, raw submissions, segments, campaign email definitions, nurturing, and attribution. |
| CRM       | Own canonical sales Leads, qualification, Sales Enquiries, assignments, calls, tasks, and sales follow-up.                             |
| Core      | Own shared contact, company, and product identity. Read and resolve through public contracts.                                          |
| Sites     | Own landing pages, public page content, and form presentation. Marketing owns capture definitions and submissions.                     |
| Mail      | Own email credentials, provider adapters, transport messages, delivery queue, and email delivery events.                               |
| Chat      | Own existing internal chat and its Telegram transport. Reuse or extend a public transport contract without a second webhook consumer.  |
| Billing   | Own quotations, orders, invoices, receipts, payments, and financial outcomes.                                                          |
| Platform  | Own tenant resolution, identity, activation, RBAC, file infrastructure, queue infrastructure, and audit infrastructure.                |
| Zetro     | Own AI providers, grants, tools, and AI action approval. Marketing owns campaign business rules.                                       |

These are target contracts, not claims that integrations already exist.
Phase 0 planning reconciled CRM ownership on 2026-10-23.
Marketing owns Campaign and promotional consent. CRM owns sales Leads and service-contact preferences. Mail owns email delivery-safety suppression.
See the [Phase 0 contract and gap review](agent/PLAN.md#phase-0-decisions-and-contract-review) for dependencies and unverified account readiness.
Existing Sites, TConnect, Sportsclub, and Ecommerce records keep their current owners.

## User experience

Use the CRM Enquiry list, show, and upsert interaction pattern with shared `@codexsun/ui` primitives.
Match the product's spacing, typography, table density, button hierarchy, badges, form validation, and activity presentation.
Customize fields, tabs, channel previews, calendar views, and relevant actions inside Marketing.
Do not copy private CRM components or force unrelated CRM actions into Marketing records.

See the [screen and action contract](agent/PLAN.md#screen-and-action-contract) for required buttons and states.
