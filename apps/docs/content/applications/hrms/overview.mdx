## Core business

Only the authenticated foundation is implemented. Workforce CRUD and payroll are planned; do not claim employee data is present.

## Primary workflow

1. Enable HRMS through effective app access and seed its view permission.
2. Open Overview and Getting Started from its own desk.
3. Use verified session/tenant context to plan employee, attendance, leave, recruitment and payroll modules.
