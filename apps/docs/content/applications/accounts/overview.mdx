## Core business

Accounting leaves own their contract; do not infer full automatic financial posting from an overview or composed screen.

## Primary workflow

1. Select a company and financial year.
2. Open the accounting overview and inspect available account/ledger/journal screens.
3. Follow the module APIs for persisted records and confirm which capabilities are complete before extending posting behavior.
